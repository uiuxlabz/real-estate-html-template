Our Journey

From a Small Office to the City's Most Trusted Agency

Founded in 2009, MAKAAN began with a simple belief: everyone deserves a home that fits their life. What started as a two-person team operating from a small downtown office has grown into one of the region's most respected real estate agencies.

Over the years, we have helped more than 2,500 families buy, sell, and invest in properties across 45 neighborhoods. Our success is built on relationships, not transactions. Every client becomes part of the MAKAAN family.
